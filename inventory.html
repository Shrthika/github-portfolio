📋 Inventory Management System

A web-based Inventory Management System designed to streamline inventory operations for businesses and retail environments. The application enables users to efficiently add, edit, delete, and manage product records while tracking categories, suppliers, quantities, and pricing information. With a clean and intuitive interface, the system improves inventory visibility and helps maintain accurate stock records.

Key Features

  • Add New Products
  • Edit Product Details
  • Delete Product Records
  • Category Management
  • Supplier Information Tracking
  • Inventory Quantity Monitoring
  • Product Price Management
  • Dynamic Product Listing
  • User-Friendly Dashboard

Technology Stack

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • Local Storage
  • VS Code
